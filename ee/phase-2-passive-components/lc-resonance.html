LC resonance

An LC tank doesn't decay — it sloshes. Energy ping-pongs between the inductor's magnetic field and the capacitor's electric field at f₀ = 1/(2π√LC). Add R (and the resistance is always there) and you get Q: how many cycles before that sloshing dies out. The impedance plot is where filters, antennas, and oscillators live.

What to notice

  • f₀ = 1/(2π√LC) — Only the product LC sets the resonant frequency. Halve L and double C and you're back where you started. The individual values set characteristic impedance Z_0 = √(L/C), which sets antenna match and Q for a given loss.
  • Series resonance = short circuit — At f₀ the inductive and capacitive reactances cancel exactly (+jωL and −j/ωC sum to zero). Only the loss R remains, so impedance drops to a minimum. This is how a series-LC trap shorts an interfering frequency to ground.
  • Parallel resonance = open circuit — Same cancellation, opposite topology: a parallel tank rejects f₀ (huge impedance) while letting other frequencies through. This is the "tank circuit" in oscillators and tube/RF amps.
  • Q sharpens both — Q = ω₀L/R = (1/R)√(L/C). High Q ⇒ narrow, peaky resonance (good for selectivity, bad for bandwidth). Crystal Q can be 10⁵; air-core RF coil Q ≈ 100; a wound power inductor at 100 kHz might be Q ≈ 30. Slide Q and watch the peak/notch grow or smear.
  • Energy is the real story — Far from f₀ the cap or inductor dominates. At f₀ the two store equal energy and trade it back and forth twice per RF cycle. The R only drains the running total — that's why Q ≈ "how many cycles before half the energy is gone × 2π."

Cheat sheet

FORMULAS

f₀
f₀ = 1 / (2π·√(L·C))
Q
Q = (1/R)·√(L/C)
BW
BW = f₀ / Q
V_L,V_C
V_L = V_C = Q · V_src (series, at f₀)
Z_0
Z_0 = √(L/C)

MEMORIZE

  • Series LC at f₀ — minimum |Z| (just R); acts as a short.
  • Parallel LC at f₀ — maximum |Z|; acts as an open (tank).
  • Below f₀ — series tank looks capacitive; above — inductive.
  • Crystal Q ~10⁵; air-core RF coil Q ~100; wound choke Q ~30.

COMMON PITFALLS

  • High Q = narrow BW — selective filters are hard to tune and drift with temp.
  • V_L, V_C blow up at f₀ — Q·V_src can punch through caps in a series tank.
  • Loaded vs unloaded Q — adding source/load resistance crashes Q; spec the right one.
