What to notice

  • Derate by 2× — A "1/8 W" 0805 running at 125 mW will cook itself in still air. Real designs target 50 % of rated.
  • Wire-wound is an inductor at RF — Don't use it above 100 kHz unless you've measured the parasitic L. A 10 W ceramic wire-wound can have several µH.
  • The 4th/5th band is tolerance, not multiplier — Gold/silver as the last band = tolerance; in the multiplier position they mean ×0.1 / ×0.01. Position decides meaning.
  • E-series spacing matches tolerance — E24 (5 %) and E96 (1 %) are spaced so adjacent values barely overlap their tolerance bands. That's why 4.7 k exists but not 5.0 k in E24.
  • Thin-film SMD = precision SMD — For voltage references, dividers, and op-amp feedback. Thick-film is fine for pull-ups, gate resistors, anything where ±5 % is invisible.

MEMORIZE

  • SMD size → power: 0402 = 1/16 W · 0603 = 1/10 W · 0805 = 1/8 W · 1206 = 1/4 W.
  • E-series spacing: E12 ≈ 20% steps (±10%), E24 ≈ 10% (±5%), E96 ≈ 2.5% (±1%) — spacing matches tolerance.
  • Derate by 2×: target ≤ 50% of rated power in still air.

COMMON PITFALLS

  • Wire-wound is inductive at RF — several µH parasitic; fails above ~100 kHz.
  • Gold/silver position matters — last band = tolerance; multiplier slot = ×0.1 / ×0.01.
  • Thick-film noise — excess noise scales with current; don't use in low-noise analog front-ends.
