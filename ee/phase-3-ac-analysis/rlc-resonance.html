RLC Resonance — Series & Parallel

Every RLC circuit has a special frequency f₀ = 1/(2π√LC) where the inductor's and capacitor's reactances cancel. In series, that means minimum impedance and maximum current — a sharp dip. In parallel, maximum impedance — a sharp peak. R sets the sharpness (Q). At resonance the L and C voltages can each be Q times the source.

What this teaches

Resonance is the cancellation point. Q = ω₀L/R is the quality factor — how undamped the circuit is. Bandwidth BW = f₀/Q is the −3 dB width of the peak/dip. Voltage magnification in series resonance: |V_L| = |V_C| = Q · |V_source| while the source only sees R. This is how crystal radios and antenna matching networks deliver "free" voltage gain.

Current I is the reference (along +Re). V_R is in phase, V_L leads by 90°, V_C lags by 90°. At resonance V_L and V_C are equal-and-opposite and cancel — V_source = V_R alone, and yet V_L = V_C = Q · V_source is sitting across each reactive element.

What to notice

  • Series and parallel mirror each other — same f₀, opposite shape. Series is a notch on the impedance and a peak on the current; parallel is the opposite. They're duals.
  • Lower R → sharper response → higher Q — and a narrower bandwidth. Try R=1 Ω vs R=100 Ω with L and C fixed and watch the −3dB markers slide in or out.
  • f₀ depends only on L·C product — push C down, f₀ shoots up (1/√LC). R doesn't move the centerline.
  • Voltage magnification is real and dangerous — at Q=20 with a 5V source, you have 100V across the cap. This is exactly why high-Q resonant circuits need rated parts and why tuned-circuit accidents kill people.
  • Off resonance, the L and C phasors no longer cancel — drag the source-frequency slider away from f₀ and watch V_L (or V_C) dominate. The net reactive voltage rotates the source phasor away from V_R.
  • At exactly f₀ in series, the circuit looks purely resistive — current and source-voltage phasors are colinear (PF=1). This is the matching condition used by antenna tuners.

Cheat sheet

FORMULAS

f₀
f₀ = 1 / (2π√LC)
Q
Q = ω₀L/R = (1/R)√(L/C)
BW
BW = f₀ / Q

MEMORIZE

  • Series — min |Z| = R at f₀ (notch).
  • Parallel R∥L∥C — max |Z| = R at f₀ (what the explorer shows). For a tank with R in series with L (transformed to parallel equivalent), max |Z| ≈ R·Q² instead — different topology.

COMMON PITFALLS

  • V_L = V_C = Q·V_src — voltage magnification can blow caps; rate parts for Q·V_src.
