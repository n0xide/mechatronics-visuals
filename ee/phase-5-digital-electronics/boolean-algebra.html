Boolean Algebra

Eight identities you must internalize. De Morgan's theorems shown as gate substitution. SOP / POS form generator for any 3-variable function.

Boolean algebra is the algebra of true / false, or in hardware, HIGH / LOW. The identities below let you simplify a logic expression before it ever becomes silicon — fewer terms means fewer gates, lower power, shorter critical path. De Morgan's theorems specifically explain why a single gate type (NAND or NOR) is enough to build everything.

Eight fundamental identities

Identity
A · 1 = A | A + 0 = A
ANDing with 1 or ORing with 0 has no effect.
Null
A · 0 = 0 | A + 1 = 1
ANDing with 0 forces 0; ORing with 1 forces 1.
Idempotent
A · A = A | A + A = A
A variable ANDed/ORed with itself is itself.
Complement
A · Ā = 0 | A + Ā = 1
A variable and its inverse can never both be true.
Involution
¬(¬A) = A
Double negation cancels.
Commutative
A·B = B·A | A+B = B+A
Order of operands does not matter.
Associative
(A·B)·C = A·(B·C)
(A+B)+C = A+(B+C)
Grouping does not matter.
Distributive
A·(B+C) = A·B + A·C
A+(B·C) = (A+B)·(A+C)
AND distributes over OR, and vice versa.

What to notice

  • Identities come in dual pairs — every AND-rule has an OR-rule with 0/1 swapped. This duality is why the algebra has half as many facts as it looks like.
  • De Morgan is the operator-swap trick — to push a NOT through, flip · ↔ + and complement each operand. This is the workhorse of gate-library substitution.
  • NAND alone is universal — that is why 74-series and CMOS libraries are full of NAND. Routing a single cell type is cheaper than mixing AND/OR/NOT.
  • SOP fits when F has few 1s, POS fits when F has few 0s — pick the canonical form that gives the shorter expression.
  • Canonical ≠ minimal — the SOP/POS expressions shown here are not optimized. K-maps (next visual) reduce them.

MEMORIZE

  • NAND is universal — every gate buildable from NAND alone (NOR too).
  • Precedence — NOT > AND > OR. Parenthesize when mixing.
  • Bubble pushing — De Morgan in symbol form: invert gate type, flip bubbles.

COMMON PITFALLS

  • ! vs ~ — in C, !x is logical (0/1), ~x is bitwise NOT.
  • = vs == — Boolean equality, not assignment.
  • "A and not B" — write A·B̄, not ¬(A·B).
