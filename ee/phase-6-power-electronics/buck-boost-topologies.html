Buck-Boost & Family — Topology Comparison

Seven DC/DC topologies side by side. Pick "I need V_out _ V_in" — the matching topology highlights.

The buck and boost are the two simplest switched-mode converters, but real systems often need more: outputs that cross V_in, isolation between input and output, negative supplies, or extreme step ratios. This page shows the common topologies, their V_out vs D formulas, and what each one is actually used for. Use the picker to filter by need.

What to notice

  • Buck-boost is a family, not one circuit — pick by polarity, isolation needs, and how V_out relates to V_in. Don't reach for a SEPIC if a boost will do.
  • Switch count grows with capability — buck (1), boost (1), inv BB (1), SEPIC (1+1 inductor), 4-switch BB (4), flyback (1 + transformer). More parts = more loss, more cost, more complexity.
  • Polarity reversal is mechanical, not magical — the inverting BB and Ćuk store energy in the inductor while the input current flows one way, then release it the other way. The diode + ground rearrangement does the polarity flip.
  • Isolation costs efficiency — flyback typically hits 75–85% versus 90%+ for non-isolated. You pay for the safety barrier in transformer copper loss and leakage inductance ringing.
  • SEPIC's coupling cap is the secret — Cs forces equal DC voltage across the two inductors and provides AC coupling for energy transfer. It must be a low-ESR film or ceramic; electrolytic will dissipate the ripple as heat.
  • Wide V_in problem — when V_in could be 3.0 V or 5.5 V and V_out is 5.0 V, you need a topology that crosses. 4-switch buck-boost gives best efficiency; SEPIC is simpler but lower η.

MEMORIZE

  • Inverting BB — V_out polarity is negative.
  • 4-switch BB — seamlessly crosses V_in = V_out.
  • Ćuk — continuous current at both ports, low EMI.

COMMON PITFALLS

  • Flyback ≠ regular xfmr — it's a coupled inductor storing energy in the gap.
  • SEPIC inductor pairing — mismatched L1/L2 (or shared core) shifts the operating point; use spec'd coupled inductors.
