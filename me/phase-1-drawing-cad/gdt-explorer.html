What this teaches

One FCF replaces a dozen ±X dimensions. The symbol says what is controlled, the value says how tight, the modifier links size to bonus tolerance, and the datums say relative to what. The MMC bonus shows why a slightly oversized hole still passes inspection — a real concern on FDM prints where holes spread inconsistently.

What to notice

  • Five fields, one box. The FCF reads strictly left to right: symbol, tolerance value with Ø prefix (cylindrical zone), modifier, then datums in priority order A | B | C.
  • Position needs datums; flatness/cylindricity don't. Switch the symbol to ⌒ flatness or ⌓ cylindricity — those control the surface against itself, so the datum cells go empty (the rule is baked in).
  • Ⓜ MMC buys bonus tolerance. At MMC (Ø2.9, the smallest hole = max material) the zone is exactly 0.20 mm. Push the hole to 3.0 mm and the zone grows to 0.30 mm — the screw still passes because the bigger hole already gives the screw more room.
  • Ⓛ LMC is the inverse. Bonus tolerance grows as the feature departs from LMC (least material). For a hole, that means bonus grows as the hole shrinks; for a pin, as the pin gets fatter. Used where minimum-material wall thickness must be guaranteed (e.g., the wall around a pin hole must keep enough meat to take load).
  • The position case fails when the hole is small AND offset. If the hole is at MMC (2.9) and the axis sits at the edge of the 0.2 mm zone, the screw (2.5) barely clears — that's the design margin baked into the spec.
  • Real FDM prints: include Ⓜ on every clearance hole. Printed holes spread inconsistently; the bonus tolerance absorbs the variation without redesign.

Cheat sheet

MEMORIZE

  • Datum A — primary, locks 3 DOF.
  • Datum B — secondary, locks 2 DOF.
  • Datum C — tertiary, locks 1 DOF.
  • Symbols — ⌖ position, ⊥ perpendicular, Ⓜ MMC, Ⓛ LMC.

MMC BONUS

Bonus
b = actual − MMC
Eff. zone
t_eff = t_FCF + b

COMMON PITFALLS

  • Datum order matters — swap A and B → different inspection result.
  • MMC needs FoS — bonus only valid on a feature-of-size, not a planar surface.
