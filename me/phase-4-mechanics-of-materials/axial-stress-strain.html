Axial Stress & Strain

Pull on a bar. The cross-section feels a uniform normal stress σ = F/A. The whole bar stretches by δ = FL/(AE). Hooke's law σ = Eε ties them together. Stress, strain, and deformation are three views of the same elastic story.

Thermal strain (no mechanical load)

Heat a bar by ΔT. If free, it lengthens by ε_T·L. If clamped between rigid walls, it can't lengthen — so it pushes back with a compressive stress σ = −E·α·ΔT.

What to notice

  • σ = F/A is an average. True over the bar's bulk, but near the loaded ends the stress is non-uniform — fibers right next to the bolt feel concentrated stress, fibers in between don't.
  • Saint-Venant's principle: beyond roughly one cross-section width from the point of load application, the stress distribution is uniform. The orange shaded zones are the "don't trust σ=F/A" regions.
  • Stiffness AE/L — the bar acts like a spring with k = AE/L. Stiffer material (E↑), fatter cross-section (A↑), or shorter bar (L↓) all reduce stretch under the same load.
  • δ scales linearly with F in the elastic region. Double the load → double the stretch. Beyond yield (not modeled here), behavior is non-linear.
  • Thermal strain is independent of E. A free bar expands by ε = α·ΔT no matter how stiff the material is. Stiffness only matters once the bar is constrained — then σ = −E·α·ΔT, and a *stiffer* material develops *more* thermal stress for the same ΔT.
  • Aluminum's α is roughly 2× steel's, so an Al standoff inside a heated cyberdeck stretches twice as much as a steel one — important when you bolt dissimilar materials together (galvanic mismatch + thermal-strain mismatch).

Cheat sheet

FORMULAS

stress
σ = F / A
strain
ε = δ / L
elongation
δ = F·L / (A·E)
thermal ε
ε_T = α · ΔT
thermal σ
σ = −E · α · ΔT(if constrained)

MEMORIZE

  • Saint-Venant — σ uniform beyond ~1 cross-section width from load.
  • α_steel ≈ 12e-6 /°C · α_Al ≈ 23e-6 /°C.
  • k = AE/L — bar is a spring.

COMMON PITFALLS

  • δ=FL/AE near a discontinuity — invalid; use FEA or stress conc factor.
  • Thermal stress with no constraint — bar just expands; σ_T = 0.
