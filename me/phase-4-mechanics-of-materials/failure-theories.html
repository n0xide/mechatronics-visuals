Mohr's Circle & Failure Theories

Any 2D stress state (σ_x, σ_y, τ_xy) can be rotated to a coordinate system where shear vanishes — the principal axes. Mohr's circle is a graphical map of every rotated state. Then ductile and brittle materials have different rules for when "enough stress" means yield or fracture: Von Mises, Tresca, and Modified Mohr.

Failure criteria comparison

Three classical theories. Von Mises (distortion energy) is the most accurate for ductile metals — the gold standard in FEA. Tresca is slightly more conservative (always predicts yielding sooner). Modified Mohr is the right tool for brittle materials (cast iron, ceramics, resin prints) that fail in tension at a different stress than in compression.

What to notice

  • Mohr's circle is geometry, not physics. Center = (σ_x + σ_y)/2, radius = √(((σ_x − σ_y)/2)² + τ_xy²). The circle plots every rotated coordinate system, so you can read off principal stresses (where it crosses the σ axis) and max shear (= radius).
  • Principal stresses come for free. σ_1 = center + radius, σ_2 = center − radius. Maximum shear stress τ_max = radius = (σ_1 − σ_2)/2.
  • Von Mises ellipse is the safe envelope for ductile metals. σ_VM = √(σ_x² − σ_x·σ_y + σ_y² + 3τ_xy²). When σ_VM ≥ S_y, the material yields. In (σ_1, σ_2) space, this traces a rotated ellipse.
  • Tresca is the inscribed hexagon — always inside the Von Mises ellipse, so always predicts failure first. Use it when you want a conservative quick check by hand.
  • Pure shear is the worst case for Tresca-vs-Von Mises disagreement. At pure shear (σ_x=σ_y=0, τ ≠ 0), Tresca says you yield at τ = S_y/2, but Von Mises lets you go to τ = S_y/√3 — about 15% more.
  • Modified Mohr for brittle: a brittle material is much weaker in tension than compression. The failure envelope is asymmetric — large negative principal stress is okay, but small positive tension fractures it. Resin prints, cast iron, ceramics: don't pull on them.
  • Factor of safety n = S_y / σ_eff. If n < 1, the part fails. n ≥ 2 is typical for static loads, ≥ 3 for fatigue.

Cheat sheet

FORMULAS

Mohr center
C = (σ_x + σ_y) / 2
Mohr radius
R = √[((σ_x − σ_y)/2)² + τ_xy²]
principals
σ_1,2 = C ± R · τ_max = R
Von Mises
σ_VM = √(σ_x² − σ_x·σ_y + σ_y² + 3τ²)
Tresca
σ_1 − σ_2 ≥ S_y

MEMORIZE

  • Ductile → Von Mises (accurate) or Tresca (conservative).
  • Brittle → modified Mohr — VM/Tresca don't apply.
  • τ_max = R — radius of Mohr's circle directly.

COMMON PITFALLS

  • Tresca over-predicts failure — that's why it's the "safer" design choice.
  • Never use VM/Tresca on brittle materials — cast iron, ceramics, glass need Mohr-type criteria.
