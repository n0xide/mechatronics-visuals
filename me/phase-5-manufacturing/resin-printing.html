Resin printing — MSLA exposure calibration

Resin printing trades layer-line visibility for a different problem: the right exposure time is a knife-edge between delamination (too little UV) and bloated, lost detail (too much). Calibration prints exist for exactly this reason. Drag the slider to see how exposure shapes a small feature row.

Layer thickness vs print time

For a 100 mm tall print on an MSLA (whole layer cured at once):

Layer heightLayers (100mm)Exposure/layerTotal print timeDetail
0.025 mm4000~2.0 s~3 h 30 minhighest (jewelry, miniatures)
0.05 mm2000~2.5 s~1 h 50 minpractical sweet spot
0.1 mm1000~3.5 s~1 h 5 minfunctional parts, fastest

Thinner layers need slightly less exposure per layer (less resin to cure), but the total layer count dominates — print time scales nearly linearly with 1/layer-height.

Hollowing & drain holes

Large solid resin prints waste resin and trap pressure. Hollow with 1.5–2 mm walls, then add drain holes: ≥2 at the lowest point during printing, 1 at the top for pressure equalization. Without drains, uncured resin pools inside the hollow and crashes the FEP film off the vat.

What to notice

  • Under-exposed prints delaminate — layers separate because UV light didn't penetrate deep enough to bond the new layer to the one below. Caused by too-short exposure, weak LCD, or cloudy resin.
  • Over-exposed prints lose fine detail — UV bleeds laterally beyond the masked area, bloating columns and washing out small text. The narrowest features die first.
  • Optimal exposure varies per bottle. Resin age, pigment, and ambient temperature all shift the ideal. Always run an AmeraLabs Town or R_E_R_F test print on a new bottle.
  • 0.05 mm is the sweet spot — half the layer count of 0.025 mm, but with detail still excellent. Reserve 0.025 mm for jewellery and miniatures.
  • Hollow + drain or don't hollow at all. A hollow shell with no drain holes guarantees a resin-trap blowout — uncured resin escapes mid-print and the FEP film pulls free of the vat.

Cheat sheet

MEMORIZE

  • Layer: 0.025–0.1 mm (sweet spot 0.05)
  • Bottom exposure = 4–10× normal
  • Min wall: 1.5 mm hollow / 2 mm robust

DECISION

  • Calibrate with AmeraLabs Town test
  • Nitrile gloves + ventilation always
  • Cure uncured waste before disposal

PITFALLS

  • Over-cure = brittle
  • Under-cure = tacky / weak
  • Hollow with no drain = trapped resin pocket
