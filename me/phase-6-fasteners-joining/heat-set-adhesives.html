Heat-set inserts & threadlockers

Two assembly tricks that make plastic parts and mass-produced bolted joints actually hold together: (a) brass heat-set inserts melt into a printed boss so the screw threads into metal, not plastic; (b) anaerobic threadlockers cure inside the thread interface to resist vibration loosening.

Application steps

  1. Clean threads with IPA, let dry
  2. Apply 1–2 drops on male thread near engagement zone
  3. Assemble and torque immediately
  4. Fixture time ~10 min, functional cure ~3–6 h, full cure 24 h (Loctite 243 TDS)

What to notice

  • Insert temperature is a band, not a setpoint. Too cold: the boss won't melt enough, insert stops short, leaves a gap that voids pull-out strength. Too hot: melt zone gets wide, plastic flows around the insert and may leave it loose or angled.
  • Heat-set in nylon is hard. Nylon's higher melt temp means tip needs ~240°C; the melt zone is narrow and the surface tends to bubble/scorch. Pull-out is high if done right but failure rate is significant.
  • Pull-out scales with boss diameter MORE than insert length for FDM. The failure mode is the boss splitting, not the threads stripping — so a thicker wall around the insert is the highest-leverage change.
  • Blue 243 is the default threadlocker. Removable, medium strength, won't cause headaches. Reserve red for things you'll never touch again.
  • Don't use red on small bolts you might service. Removing red 271 needs 250°C — for an M2 or M3, you risk twisting the bolt off before it breaks free. Use blue and accept the trade.
  • Green wicks INTO an assembled joint. Apply on the side of the bolt-nut interface; capillary action pulls it in. The only option when you already torqued and forgot.

Cheat sheet

MEMORIZE — heat-set

  • Tip temp: PETG 200–220°C
  • Tip temp: PLA 180–200°C
  • Tip temp: PA12 240°C
  • Boss OD = insert OD + 0.0–0.1 mm
  • Pull-out M2.5/PETG/4mm ≈ 200–400 N

DECISION — threadlocker

  • Blue 243 — medium, removable (default)
  • Red 271 — permanent, needs heat
  • Purple 222 — small bolts <M6
  • Green 290 — wicks into assembled joint

PITFALLS

  • Over-torque heat-set in PETG → boss pulls out, not insert
  • Threadlocker full cure = 24 h (functional ~3–6 h, fixture ~10 min)
  • Red on M2/M3 → bolt twists off before releasing
