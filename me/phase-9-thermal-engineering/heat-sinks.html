Heat sinks — fin efficiency, decay, sizing

A fin is just an extended surface — but only the part near the base sees the full temperature rise. The tip cools faster than the base, so the average ΔT (driving heat into air) is lower than you'd naively expect. Fin efficiency η_f = tanh(mL)/(mL) captures this, where m = √(2h/(k·t)). What to notice

  • Fin efficiency falls as fins get longer or thinner. Past mL ≈ 1.5, you're adding aluminum that mostly sits near ambient — wasted material. The first few mm of fin do most of the work.
  • More fins, shorter, beats fewer fins, taller. Total heat removed = η_f · A_fin · h · ΔT_base. Lots of short fins keep η_f near 1; a few tall ones drop η_f and waste area.
  • Forced convection changes the optimum. When h jumps from 10 → 50, the optimal fin gets shorter (because heat now leaves faster, so the base-to-tip drop is steeper). Fan-cooled sinks have tight, narrow fins; passive sinks are fewer and chunkier.
  • Conductor matters at the base. A copper fin has higher η_f than an aluminum fin of identical geometry — heat carries further down the fin before air cools it. Copper sinks are denser and cost more, but earn back at high power.
  • Orientation matters for natural convection. Vertical fins set up a chimney → air rises naturally → h effectively higher. Horizontal-fin sinks trap hot air and lose ~30 % of the natural-convection h. Mount fins vertical when there's no fan.
  • If you need Θ_sa < 5 °C/W in still air, you're going to need a big sink or a fan. Below ~3 °C/W passive you're at the limits of natural convection in a normal-sized enclosure. Add airflow.

Cheat sheet

FORMULAS

fin efficiency
η_f = tanh(mL) / (mL)m = √(2h / (k·t))
required
R_sa = (Tj_max − T_amb)/P − R_jc − R_cs

MEMORIZE / DECIDE

  • Natural R_sa ~ 10–20 °C/W small sink; forced 2–5 °C/W.
  • Vertical fins for passive (chimney effect).
  • Anodize black to add radiation contribution.
  • R_sa < 5 °C/W → fan.

COMMON PITFALLS

  • Horizontal fins trap hot air — loses ~30 % h.
  • Too thick / too long fins — η_f drops, wasted metal.
  • Forgot R_jc + R_cs — sink alone doesn't set Tj.
