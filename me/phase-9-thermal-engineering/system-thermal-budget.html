System thermal budget — enclosure-level

Every component dumps heat into the same enclosure. The internal air rises above external ambient by an amount proportional to total power times the enclosure's thermal resistance. Then each component sits at Tj ≈ T_internal + P · Θ_ja. (Note: datasheet Θ_ja already assumes a still-air ambient per JEDEC, so applying it to the enclosure's interior temperature double-counts some of the air rise — treat the result as a worst-case sizing number.) Sweep the inputs, watch the tightest-margin component turn red, then try a mitigation: vent the case, add a sink, derate, or switch material. What to notice

  • Internal ambient is the silent killer. An enclosure with Θ_enc = 8 °C/W full of 1.5 W of electronics warms its own air by 12 °C — before any component-specific heating. Components see 42 °C ambient even when the room is 30 °C.
  • The component with the lowest Tj_max usually loses first. An ILI9488 display (Tj_max ≈ 70 °C) breaks before an RP2350 (85 °C) or TPS61023 (125 °C). Plan around the weakest member.
  • LiPo cells aren't "components" but they care about ambient. Keep their cell temp below 45 °C for cycle-life. They have no Tj_max in the IC sense — they have a lifetime-vs-temperature curve, and they age faster the hotter they sit.
  • Venting beats heatsinking at the enclosure level. A few well-placed slots drop Θ_enc from 8 to ~3 (a 5 °C drop in internal air for 1.5 W). That's cheaper than every IC-level sink combined.
  • Worst-case stacks. Hot room (40 °C) + sealed enclosure + heavy load + dusty TIM = catastrophic. Design at worst-case, not nominal. 15 °C of headroom is the usual rule.
  • Mitigation order: vent → derate → IC sinks. Vents are cheap and improve every component at once. IC-level sinks help one component and add mass and assembly cost.

Cheat sheet

FORMULAS

internal amb
T_int = T_ext + P_total · R_enc
junction
Tj = T_amb + P · R_ja

MEMORIZE / DECIDE

  • Margin ≥ 15–20 °C Tj_max − Tj at worst case.
  • Sealed R_enc ~ 5–10 °C/W·W; vented ~ 3.
  • LiPo < 45 °C — lifetime halves per +10 °C above 25.
  • Mitigation order: vent → heatsink → fan → derate.

COMMON PITFALLS

  • Budgeting at nominal — worst case stacks max amb + load + aged TIM.
  • Weakest Tj_max wins — display (70 °C) breaks before MCU (85 °C).
  • Forgot enclosure rise — components see internal air, not room.
